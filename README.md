AI-powered football scouting platform. Players upload highlights, AI generates scouting reports, clubs and scouts discover talent. Built for African football visibility.
# ⚽ Scout Me
### DISCOVER. ANALYZE. GET SCOUTED.

> AI-powered football scouting platform built to give talented players — especially across Africa — the visibility they deserve.

---

## What is Scout Me?

Scout Me is a mobile app that connects football players with clubs, academies, and scouts using AI analysis and a social-style discovery feed.

Players upload their highlights. AI breaks down their playstyle. Scouts find the talent. Simple.

---

## The Problem

Thousands of talented footballers across Africa never get seen — not because they aren't good enough, but because the right people never watched them play. Traditional scouting is limited by geography, connections, and money.

Scout Me fixes that.

---

## Core Features

### 🟢 For Players
- Create a football profile (position, age, location, dominant foot, bio)
- Upload match highlights and footage
- AI-generated playstyle analysis and scouting card
- Skill ratings across: Pace, Dribbling, Passing, Shooting, Physical, Football IQ
- Weekly trending rankings by position
- Profile verification system
- Player comparison system

### 🔍 For Clubs & Scouts
- Discover players through filters (position, age, location, skill level)
- AI-generated scouting reports
- Save and bookmark talent
- Direct messaging and contact system
- Verified scout badge system

---

## App Flow

```
Splash Screen → Select Account Type (Player / Club)
             → Sign up with Google or Email
             → Player: Build profile + upload highlights
             → Club: Browse feed + filter + scout
```
