How It Works
TWO SIDES.
ONE PLATFORM.

Whether you're a player trying to get noticed or a club searching for the next star — Scout Me was built for you.

⚽
FOR PLAYERS
Get Discovered
01
Build Your Profile
Add your position, age, location, dominant foot and a short bio. Your digital scouting card.
02
Upload Your Highlights
Drop your best match footage. Show the world what you can do on the pitch.
03
AI Analyzes Your Game
Our AI breaks down your playstyle and generates a full scouting report — Pace, Dribbling, Passing, Shooting, Physical, Football IQ.
04
Get Seen by Scouts
Appear in rankings and discovery feeds. Scouts and clubs find you directly.
🔍
FOR CLUBS & SCOUTS
Find the Next Star
01
Create a Scout Account
Sign up as a club or verified scout. Get access to the full talent discovery platform.
02
Filter & Search
Search by position, age, location, skill rating, and more. Find exactly who you're looking for.
03
Read AI Scouting Reports
Every player comes with an AI-generated report. Strengths, weaknesses, playstyle summary — all in one place.
04
Contact & Sign Talent
Message players directly. Bookmark your favourites. Build your shortlist.
